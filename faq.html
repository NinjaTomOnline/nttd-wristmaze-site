Frequently Asked Questions

What is the game style?

It is a watch-only tilt maze game focused on short sessions with escalating hazard-driven chambers.

Do I need Game Center?

No. Leaderboard and achievement syncing are optional. If signed out, clears queue locally.

Can I use Large Text or Minimal HUD?

Yes. These are in Watch Settings and are tuned for low-readability and quick glanceability.

Will this run on iPhone?

WristMaze is a watch-only product. No iPhone gameplay companion is required.

Do complications work?

Yes. They surface progress and streak context, then deep-link into the app screens.
