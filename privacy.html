Privacy Policy

Updated November 13, 2026

NinjaTom Tilt Dojo: Wrist Maze Challenge uses motion from your Apple Watch to steer the ball. The game processes motion on your watch during play and calibration.

What the App Stores

  • Story progression, best runs, streaks, settings, and comfort preferences on-device.
  • Shared progress snapshots for complication surfaces.
  • Queued Game Center progress while signed out.
  • Your Game Center alias and sync status, for display in the app.

What the App Sends

  • If you sign in to Game Center, the app submits leaderboard scores and achievement progress to Apple.
  • See Apple’s Game Center privacy notice for how Apple handles that data.

Tracking

Wrist Maze does not track you across apps or websites. It contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.

User Controls

  • Play without Game Center sign-in.
  • Remove the app from your Apple Watch to remove its local app data. Game Center data is managed separately by Apple.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@ninjatomapps.com.
