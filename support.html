Wrist Maze Challenge Support

Wrist Maze runs on Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later.

Game Center

  • Sign in to Game Center in Apple’s system settings, then reopen Wrist Maze.
  • Open Progress in Wrist Maze and use the Game Center controls to retry syncing queued scores.

Gameplay and Controls

  • If the ball moves while your wrist is still, open Settings and choose Recalibrate Tilt.
  • Choose another tilt preset in Settings if steering feels too sensitive or too slow.

Complications and Progress

  • Open Wrist Maze once after an update to refresh your progress on the watch face.
  • If a Daily Dojo, streak, or progress complication is still stale, remove and re-add it from your watch face.
